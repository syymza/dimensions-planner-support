Privacy

Effective 6 November 2026. Updated 9 November 2026 to explain calculator storage and the optional picture features introduced in version 1.3.

Picture import and portable picture backups below apply to version 1.3. Earlier versions use numeric workspaces and JSON backups.

Provider and contact

Dimensions Planner is a syymza app. Privacy/support contact: daniele@duck.com.

Information kept on your device

Source dimensions, target canvas dimensions, placement choices, saved preset names, project names, display preferences, and the last valid calculator ratio and dimension inputs are stored locally. If you add a picture, the app also stores a local preview and its source pixel dimensions and orientation for use in Calculator, Planner and saved projects.

There is no in-app account, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated backend. Plans and picture previews are not automatically transmitted to the developer. The app does not track you across apps or websites.

Pictures you select

You can choose a JPEG, PNG or HEIC/HEIF still image through Apple's Photos picker or a Files provider. The app uses the system selection picker rather than requesting access to your entire photo library. A cloud-only selection may need to be downloaded by Photos or your Files provider under that provider's settings and terms before the app can import it.

The app reads the selected image's pixel dimensions and display orientation on your device. It creates a local PNG preview with a maximum edge of 2,048 pixels, normalized for display orientation and sRGB color. The original source dimensions are stored separately from the preview size. The app does not copy the original image's EXIF location or camera metadata into the preview. Previews imported from portable backups are also reprocessed before storage to remove source metadata.

The app does not modify the original photo or retain the original image file as part of the workspace. Rotation changes the preview and planning dimensions, not the original photo. Once imported, the local preview can be used offline. Pictures are not sent to the developer or an image-analysis service. The app does not infer real-world measurements.

Sharing and portable backups

Share specs remains text-only. It provides the current dimensions, scale and fit/crop measurements to a destination you choose through Apple's share sheet. It does not export an edited picture.

For a readable workspace, a normal portable .dimensionsplanner backup contains saved plans, the last valid canvas, calculator inputs and the picture previews referenced by that workspace. It does not contain original photo files. Older JSON backups remain importable; they do not include picture files.

Exported files do not have encryption added by this app. The Files provider you select may sync them under its own settings and terms. The app does not automatically send an export to the developer. Picture previews can show personal content even when source metadata has been removed, so review the destination before sharing a backup.

Emergency recovery exports

If a workspace cannot be read safely, such as because it is corrupt or uses an unsupported format, Export original file exports the untouched workspace JSON only. This emergency JSON export does not include pictures and is not a complete photo backup. The app shows a warning before opening Files.

Picture files remain in the app's local storage while the workspace is protected. Keep the app installed so those files remain available for recovery; the emergency export alone cannot preserve them. This does not guarantee recovery. Normal backups from a readable workspace include the referenced previews as described above.

Device backups

Apple device backups may include app data, including local picture previews, according to your device and iCloud settings. App-managed automatic cloud sync is not implemented. Device backups and provider-held exports are managed separately from local app storage.

Retention, deletion and Undo

Deleting a preset removes its saved record. The latest deletion is temporarily retained in memory for Undo during the current app session; it is not a persistent recovery archive. Deleting a preset does not clear the current canvas.

Removing or replacing a picture affects the current canvas. Saved plans retain the previews they reference. Unused previews are kept during the session to support Undo. On a subsequent launch, after a readable workspace is loaded, the app attempts to remove preview files that are no longer referenced. Previews referenced by the current canvas or any saved plan are kept. A protected or corrupt workspace does not trigger this cleanup.

Removing the app removes its local storage. Earlier exports and device backups can contain previously deleted presets or picture previews. Importing an old backup can restore saved plans and any previews it includes. Manage exported copies through Files, the chosen provider or Apple's backup settings.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email support, your email address and the information you include are handled by the email providers involved and used to respond to your request. The app's mail link supplies a product-name subject, without automatically attaching plans, pictures, backups or diagnostics. Include only information you choose to provide. Contact the support address for questions about support correspondence or deletion requests concerning it.

Website hosting

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, including visitor IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement. These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or cookies set by this site.
