Support for Dimensions Planner, the pixel-dimension and aspect-ratio planner for iPhone and iPad.

What it does

Calculate dimensions from an aspect ratio, compare fit with centered fill/crop, and keep named presets by project. The app does not export edited image files, infer real-world object sizes or modify display/game settings.

In version 1.3, you can also choose a picture from Photos or Files to read its pixel dimensions and preview it in Calculator, Planner and saved projects. The original photo is not changed.

Quick example

A 4032 × 3024 source in a 1920 × 1080 canvas fits at 1440 × 1080, leaving 240 canvas pixels on each side. Fill & crop keeps 75% of the source area and crops 378 original source pixels from the top and bottom.

Editing dimensions

Enter ungrouped values from 0.001 to 10000000. Locale decimal marks, dot decimals and Arabic decimal digits/marks are supported. Grouping separators are rejected. A target ratio lock updates the paired dimension. Finish an edit with Done; valid edits also save when leaving the app. Incomplete or invalid text does not replace the previous completed draft.

Pictures in version 1.3

Choose Add picture in Calculator or Planner, then Photos or Files. JPEG, PNG and HEIC/HEIF still images are supported. A cloud-only picture may need its provider to download it first. The app reads the selected picture rather than requesting access to your whole photo library.

Fit shows the whole source; Fill & crop shows a centered crop. Rotate, replace or remove the preview without editing the original photo. Calculator inputs change when you choose Use picture’s ratio. Save to project keeps the plan’s preview and rotation.

The app stores a local preview up to 2,048 pixels on its longest edge, plus the original pixel dimensions. It does not keep the original image file. Imported previews work offline. Files are limited to 100 MB and 200 megapixels; if an import fails, the existing plan is preserved.

Your projects

Save to project keeps source, target and placement. Search/filter presets, rename them, reorder through Edit or Move earlier/later, and confirm deletion. Undo restores the latest deletion during the session. Deleting a preset does not clear the active canvas.

Backups

In version 1.3, Export backup saves a portable .dimensionsplanner package containing saved plans, the last valid canvas, calculator inputs and the referenced picture previews. It does not contain the original photos. Import adds saved plans with previously unknown IDs while preserving existing local plans and the current canvas. Older JSON backups remain importable.

Version 1.2 and earlier use JSON workspace backups without pictures. In those versions, import files must be valid schema-1 workspaces no larger than 2 MB. Use the export format offered by your installed version. Export before removing the app. This app adds no encryption to backup files; Files providers and device backups are managed separately. Keep exported copies in a trusted location, because previews may contain personal information.

If saving fails

Keep the app installed and check available device storage. An unreadable or unsupported workspace is protected against further writes. Export original file saves its untouched JSON only. In version 1.3 this emergency export excludes pictures and is not a complete photo backup; the app warns before export and retains picture files locally while the workspace is protected. Keep the app installed so those files remain available for recovery. Recovery is not guaranteed.

Do not send a complete backup unless you choose to and it is needed for the agreed support process.

Report an issue

Include the app version/build, device and OS version, the steps taken, and what you expected. Screenshots and measurements are optional. The app does not attach plans or diagnostics automatically.
